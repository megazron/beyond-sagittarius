Privacy Policy

Beyond Sagittarius, by MegaZroN. Last updated 2026-11-19. Template, not reviewed by a lawyer.

The same text is published at https://megazron.github.io/beyond-sagittarius/privacy.html (public/privacy.html).

The game has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. I receive no personal data from it.

What is stored. Your save, settings, character choices and quality preset are kept on your device: in your browser's local storage, the desktop app's equivalent, or the Android app's own private storage. They are not uploaded. Clear your site data, start a new game, or uninstall the Android app to remove them.

The Android app. The Android app (com.megazron.beyondsagittarius) collects no data and shares no data. It asks for no permissions, not even internet access: the whole game ships inside the app and it never connects to a server. If you have Android's own device backup turned on, Android may include the app's save in your personal Google backup; that is between you and Google, and I never see it. A link in the Legal screen opens this page in your browser, outside the game.

Network requests.

  • The web version is served by GitHub Pages, and the game's files and audio load from there. GitHub's own privacy statement covers those requests, including server logs of your IP address.
  • Fonts ship with the game and load from the same place as everything else. Nothing is requested from Google or any other font service.
  • Links to GitHub open only when you tap or click them.

Device information. The game reads your language, screen size and input type on your device to choose defaults. These never leave your device.

Children. The game is made for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect anything from anyone, children included.

Changes. If this ever changes, this page changes first, with a new date at the top.

Questions. Open an issue at https://github.com/megazron/beyond-sagittarius/issues.
