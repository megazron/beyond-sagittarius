# Changelog

What changed for players, newest first. No story spoilers here.

## 1.2.0 (not released yet)

The first version I am preparing for Google Play, and a big update for the browser and Windows builds.

**A city before the stars.** The opening now starts in Zenvora, the spaceport town, as a city you can walk:
districts round the old town, cars, a tram, crowds on the pavements, shop interiors, a map and GPS, time of
day and rain. Skip it any time in the System settings, or watch the opening again from Chapters.

**People who feel alive.** Walkers step round you and each other, wait when the pavement is full and keep to
the pavement instead of the road. Nobody pops in or out of view, swaps their look or turns flat when you walk
up to them. Characters move while they talk and work, with settled hips and feet, calmer hair, and fixes to
necks, collars and clothes poking through.

**Fire.** The camp fire has layered flames that rise past the sticks, a coal bed and drifting embers.

**Coming later, not in this version's story yet.** I have built a hunting and fighting system with tools as
people really used them (hand axe, spear, spear-thrower, bow, sling, spear fishing, snares and a night at the
fire). It is in the code but the story does not call it yet, so players will not meet it in 1.2.0.

**Sound and voice.** New voice generation with direction and retakes, the player's own voice lines for either
build of the Scientist, breaths, gasps and crowd sounds, and the fights' own sounds. Every subtitle names its
speaker once.

**Runs on more devices.** Much lighter on phones: merged street meshes, fewer draw calls in the forest, lighter
shadows, people's textures freed when nobody wears them, and a memory guard that saves and moves to a lighter
preset instead of crashing. Scenes are warmed during loading so the first steps into a new place do not stutter.

**Android.** An Android app (internal testing only for now): the whole game inside the app, fully offline, no
permissions, the back button opens the pause menu, the game pauses when you leave it, and the screen stays on
while you play.

**Privacy.** The privacy policy has its own page and is linked from the Legal screen. The game still collects
nothing.

Known problems in this version are listed honestly in the release checklist before it goes out.
