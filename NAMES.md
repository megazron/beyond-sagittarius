# Names

Every name I invented is futuristic: places, items, tech, people the story names in passing, and shops.
The owner's own names are not changed: the crew (Zenvik, Draxum, Lyrra, Skyvra, Bolzar, Nuvexa), MegaZroN, Mir,
Taravel and the game's title. Parody brands keep their joke (Lamazon, Capple, Snapgram, Trapadvisor, Tiktak, Rishta.ai).
Real places in space (Europa, Ceres, Titan, Ganymede, Mars, Venus, Saturn, the galactic centre) stay real.
