# Pre-release checklist: Beyond Sagittarius 1.2.0 (Android versionCode 2)

Prepared 2026-11-23 by the release agent. This lists every gate, every open quality issue I know of and what I do in
Play Console. Green gates show the game works; they do not show it is good. Only I sign off, in `1.2.0.md`, after
playing the build myself on a phone and on desktop.

State words: **green** (gate passed on the exact commit), **open** (known problem, not fixed), **better, not fixed**
(work landed, still visible), **unknown** (not measured or not seen on a real device).

## 3. Open quality issues (from the agents' handoffs and my own complaints)

| Issue | Honest state |
| --- | --- |
| Pedestrian glitches (popping, swapping, flat cards, sliding, walking through people) | **better, not fixed on main**. Perf agent 26 measured the causes and cut them to near zero in 7-minute GPU runs on branch `ped26` (Mobile: vanish 32 to 0, swaps 41 to 0, teleports 133 to 0; Web: swaps 216 to 1, overlaps 518 to 129). That branch is NOT landed in 1.2.0 as built here. I must see it myself |
| Arms and clothes going through the body, spear through the person | **better, not fixed**. CI pose gates hold the bow, sling and spear-thrower arms out of the body; clothes still poke through in some poses (life agent's branch `life-wip` has more, not landed) |
| Phone memory about 2 GB | **open**. Real-GPU Mobile measurement: renderer 1.36 to 1.54 GB plus GPU process 2.38 GB private; on software GL about 2.5 GB. The planet world, combat player model and morph textures stay resident in the city. Risky on 4 GB phones |
| Title load about 21 s cold | **open**. A500 cold load 20.7 to 22.8 s; a shader compile fix (c148ab9, perf branch) not measured on main |
| Fire look | **better, not fixed**. Flames now rise past the sticks; the foot of the fire inside the sticks is still a bright yellow-white mass; heat shimmer hard to see at night |
| Night look | **better, not fixed**. Planetshine raised; still very dark away from the fire; animal rim light a little glassy up close |
| Walking wade rings | **open**, still visible (combat scenes only) |
| Camera inside a passer-by in Zenvora | **open** on main: in the 1.2.0 city take a walker crossed between the camera and me and the view went inside her hair for about 2 s (ped26 aims at this) |
| Camera against cars when walking in the road | **open**: seen in the bazaar take |
| Mirrored text | **open**: some shop signs read mirrored from one side; minimap street labels turn upside down |
| Touch screens show keyboard hints | **open**: phone menus show "Enter Select", "Esc Close" |
| Lag far from spawn | fixed for the GPS router (0878731); not seen on a real phone |

| Hindi and Urdu voices | about 110 lines play in English under Hindi or Urdu subtitles; no native speaker review |
| Chapter Two voices | playable; some dubbing still marked "to render" in the level notes |
| Combat system | built but the story does not call it yet: not part of 1.2.0 for players |

## 5. What I do in Play Console (when I decide to)

The full guide is `PLAYSTORE_GUIDE.md`. For this build:

1. Play the 1.2.0 build on my phone and desktop, then write my verdict in `store/review/1.2.0.md`.
2. If I have no developer account yet: section 1 of the guide (personal account, developer name MegaZroN, US$25, ID
   check, phone verification).
3. Create the app (section 2) and fill App content from `APP_CONTENT_ANSWERS.md` (section 3).
4. Main store listing: text from `listing-en.txt`, `graphics/icon-512.png`, `graphics/feature-graphic-1024x500.png`,
   phone screenshots from `screenshots-phone/`, 7-inch and 10-inch tablet screenshots from `screenshots-tablet/`.
   The trailer clip is for a YouTube link (Play takes a YouTube URL, not a file).
5. Test and release > Internal testing > Create new release: upload `BeyondSagittarius-1.2.0-2-INTERNAL-TEST.aab`, release name `1.2.0 (2)`,
   notes from `release-notes-en.txt`. Accept Play App Signing. Add myself as tester, install from the opt-in link.
6. Closed test: at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row (guide section 5), then Apply for production
   (section 6).
7. The internal-test AAB was built with `BS_INTERNAL_TEST=1`. For closed testing or production, a release build
   needs `Owner approved: yes` in `1.2.0.md`, written by me, then `store/release.sh 1.2.0` (it bumps versionCode).
