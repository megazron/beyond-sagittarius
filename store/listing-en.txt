APP NAME (max 30)
Beyond Sagittarius

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Stranded past a black hole, rebuild science from one rock. Sci-fi survival tale.

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Year 2189. A suspiciously cheap tourist route to the galactic centre ends at Sagittarius A*.

You wake up alone on a planet nobody charted, with a dying wrist device and something very large walking between the trees. The jokes come first. The fear catches up.

Then you rebuild science from nothing, one rock at a time, because the way home is a ship and the ship is several thousand years of homework away.

STORY FIRST
Six friends booked the cheap route. Now they are scattered across an alien world. Find water, make fire, build a shelter, brace a spear when the night comes, and start finding your crew.

REAL SCIENCE, IN THE RIGHT ORDER
Knap flint, twist cord, fire clay, burn lime, melt sand. One discovery per level, each with a short, true explanation in the codex. (Do not try this at home: it is simplified for a game.)

CHOICES THAT STICK
Timed decisions, quick-time moments and things you never knew counted. The Ledger keeps all of it, and MegaZroN, the narrator, reads it back to you, rudely.

SIX FRIENDS, SIX FACES
Each crew member has their own lineage, voice and bad habits. Build your own Scientist in the character creator.

ENGLISH, HINDI AND URDU
Text in all three, Urdu right to left, and voices in all three for most lines (the rest are spoken in English under Hindi or Urdu subtitles for now). Voice and subtitle languages are set separately. The voices are synthetic.

MADE FOR PHONES TOO
Touch controls with a thumbstick, look by dragging, gamepad support, and quality presets for your device.

WHAT IS IN IT TODAY
The opening in Zenvora, a spaceport city you can walk, the title sequence, Chapter One: The First 24 Hours (levels 1 to 10) and Chapter Two: Rocks to Reason (levels 11 to 21). More chapters are on the way.

FREE, FULLY OFFLINE
No ads. No in-app purchases. No account. No internet needed: the whole game is inside the app, and it collects no data.

Note: the cutscenes use bright flashes and fast cuts, and the game includes flickering light. A recent phone with good graphics is recommended.

Any brands in the game are parodies.
